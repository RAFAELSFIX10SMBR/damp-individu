About me

I've always been drawn to designing Instagram feeds with a modern, polished look, and it's the thing I'm genuinely best at. My experience comes from three committees in design, documentation and multimedia, one of which I led, which gave me a taste of both creative direction and hands-on execution.

What can I make for you

I can build a visual identity from scratch for an event or organization: guidebook covers and layouts, ID cards, lanyards, and social media content for reveals and announcements, mostly in Figma and Canva. I also edit teaser and promotional videos that build anticipation and tie the identity together across formats. What matters most to me is consistency: colors, typography and tone unified across every piece, so the result feels like one brand and not a collection of random pieces.

Feeds

I've designed reveal feeds and social media content for multiple committees, shaping how an audience forms its first impression of a team or event. It taught me how much a feed's visual consistency influences whether people engage further.

Design

Design is the core of my committee work, from guidebook covers and ID cards to lanyards, all built around one visual identity. Each project sharpened my ability to make individual pieces feel like part of a cohesive brand rather than standalone designs.

Photography
